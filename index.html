How It Works

A simple flow for both service seekers and student providers

1

Create an account

Students sign up using their school information to build trust and accountability.

2

Browse or post

Users can browse available services or post a request describing what support they need.

3

Connect safely

Students can message providers, check ratings, and agree on price, deadline, and output.

4

Review after completion

Ratings and reviews help maintain quality and encourage responsible student service.
